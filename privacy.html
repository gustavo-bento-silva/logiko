Logiko: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how Logiko: Logic Puzzles ("the app"), published by Gustavo Bento ("we"), handles your data.

Summary

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads and no advertising ID. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your puzzle progress is stored only on your device.
  • We collect pseudonymous usage analytics and crash reports (linked only to random installation IDs) to improve the game. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent first.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Data stored on your device

  • Puzzle progress, settings, streak, level and hint balance are saved locally on your device.
  • This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Analytics and crash reports

  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. Examples: puzzles started and completed, use of hints, screens viewed, including the purchase screen, purchases made, and crash reports. We also receive basic device and app information such as device model, OS version, app version, language and approximate location (derived from the IP address).
  • The data is linked to random installation identifiers generated by Firebase. It is not linked to your name, email or advertising ID, and it is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): analytics and crash reporting only start after you accept.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy. When you do, data collection stops and the installation identifier used for analytics is reset.
  • Retention: data already received stays on Google's servers, no longer linked to your current installation, until its retention period ends (up to 14 months for analytics, 90 days for crash reports).
  • Google's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

  • In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their terms and privacy policies.
  • The app only receives confirmation of what you own, so it can unlock content.
  • You can restore purchases in Settings → Restore purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Because analytics data is not linked to your identity, the simplest way to exercise these rights is to withdraw consent in Settings, which stops collection. To ask for the deletion of data already collected, contact us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
