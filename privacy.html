Logiko: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how Logiko: Logic Puzzles ("the app"), published by Gustavo Bento ("we"), handles your data.

Summary

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads and no advertising ID. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your puzzle progress is stored only on your device.
  • We collect pseudonymous usage analytics (linked only to a random installation ID) to improve the game. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent first.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.

Data stored on your device

  • Puzzle progress, settings, streak, level and hint balance are saved locally on your device.
  • This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Analytics

  • We use Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies) to understand how the game is played. Examples: puzzles started and completed, use of hints, and screens viewed, including the purchase screen. We also receive basic device and app information such as OS version, app version, country and crash or performance diagnostics.
  • The data is linked to a random installation identifier generated by Unity. It is not linked to your name, email or advertising ID, and it is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): analytics only starts after you accept.
  • Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy. When you do, data collection stops and a deletion request is sent to Unity.
  • Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Purchases

  • In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their terms and privacy policies.
  • The app only receives confirmation of what you own, so it can unlock content.
  • You can restore purchases in Settings → Restore purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Because analytics data is not linked to your identity, the simplest way to exercise these rights is to withdraw consent in Settings, which requests deletion. You can also contact us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
